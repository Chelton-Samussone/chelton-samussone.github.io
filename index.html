02

About

Architecture is the axis. Other disciplines are instruments.

My practice sits at the intersection of architecture, spatial analysis, urban planning and visual communication.

I use different tools and disciplines to understand space from different perspectives — from the scale of the building to the scale of the city.

GIS helps me analyse territory. Planning helps me understand urban systems. Design helps me communicate ideas. Architecture brings these perspectives together.
